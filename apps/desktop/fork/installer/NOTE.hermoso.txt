Hermoso
=======

Hermoso is the brother of Hermes: Hermes Desktop (Nous Research, MIT licence) with a bodyguard in
front of it. It is an unofficial fork, not affiliated with Nous Research or with Mullvad VPN AB.

What he does
  - Shield:       when the VPN tunnel drops, the agent stands still until it is back. A radar shows
                  where the app's connections really go.
  - Guardian:     before the agent does something that cannot be undone (deleting folders, rewriting
                  git history, running what it downloaded, reading keys, ...) a person decides, in
                  plain words. Two things are never allowed: wiping a disk, editing Hermoso himself.
  - Time Machine: the project is snapshotted before the agent changes files; one click rewinds, and
                  what you edited yourself is kept.
  - Ghost:        a window that keeps nothing. Its folder is deleted on the panic key
                  (Ctrl+Alt+Shift+Backspace), on close, or at the next start.
  - Teleport:     move the Mullvad exit country for a while; it comes back by itself.
  - Aura:         a glow that grows with what he did for you. Just for fun.

He is a guard, not a vault: he is not a sandbox, the guardian recognises patterns and can be fooled
by a determined program, and only Mullvad's "Always require VPN" is a real wall against a leak. What
each protection does and does not do is written in the Hermoso page and in the project's threat model.

Mullvad
  The VPN gate, Shield and Teleport need Mullvad VPN installed and an account logged in
  (https://mullvad.net): when it starts, Hermoso connects Mullvad and holds Hermes back until the
  tunnel is up. Guardian, Time Machine and Ghost do not need it, and you can start without the VPN
  for one launch ("Continue without VPN") or switch the requirement off in Settings > VPN.

The first time it starts it installs Hermes itself (a few minutes, it needs the internet). The
installer carries nobody's chats, keys or settings. If this computer already has Hermes, Hermoso
uses the same profile: close one of them before opening the other.

The installer is not signed: Windows SmartScreen says "Unknown publisher" once ("More info", then
"Run anyway"). Compare its SHA-256 with the one in the release notes if you want to be sure.

Updates are not done from inside the app: whoever gave you this sends a new installer, and installing
it over this one keeps your data. To remove it: Settings > Apps > Hermoso. Your data stays
(%LOCALAPPDATA%\hermes); delete that folder by hand if you want it gone too.
