Hermoso
=======

Hermoso is the brother of Hermes: Hermes Desktop (Nous Research, MIT licence) with a bodyguard in
front of it. It is an unofficial fork, not affiliated with Nous Research or with Mullvad VPN AB.

  - It keeps Hermes behind a Mullvad VPN tunnel: when it starts it connects Mullvad and holds
    Hermes back until the tunnel is up. You need Mullvad VPN installed and an account logged in
    (https://mullvad.net).
  - It adds protection and powers on top of that (see the project page for the list).

The first time it starts it installs Hermes itself (a few minutes, it needs the internet).
It does not carry anyone's chats, keys or settings: you start from zero.

Updates are not done from inside the app: whoever gave you this sends a new installer, and installing
it over this one keeps your data. To remove it: Settings > Apps > Hermoso. Your data stays
(%LOCALAPPDATA%\hermes); delete that folder by hand if you want it gone too.
